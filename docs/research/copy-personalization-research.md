# The lysoreach Outreach Copy Playbook
### How to generate personalized B2B copy that drives the recipient to ACT

This is the operating manual for the copy-generation engine. Every rule below is encodable. Where a stat survived fact-checking across multiple datasets, it's stated as fact; where it's a single-vendor or cross-stitched figure, it's flagged and used only directionally.

---

## 1. The Highest-Leverage Principles (ranked)

1. **Relevance is the multiplier that gates every other lever** — a signal-specific opener (a funding round, a named hire, a job post) is what separates a read from a delete; without it, no persuasion lever fires because the email is pattern-matched as spam first. *(Cross-source: ~2–2.5x reply lift for signal-based vs generic, not the inflated 5x.)*
2. **Anchor on ONE recent, public, professional trigger — not a merge tag** — `{{first_name}}`/`{{company}}` is table stakes and lifts almost nothing; the recipient already knows their own name. The mechanism is proof-of-effort: a checkable fact says "a human did homework on me."
3. **Use a soft, interest-based CTA on first touch — never a calendar ask** — asking for time demands a finite resource before trust exists; an interest question costs the reader nothing and is a micro-yes. *(Robust: Gong's 15% cold-stage booking for interest-CTAs; Puzzle Inbox's soft 4.2% vs hard 1.4% reply, ~3x.)*
4. **One idea, one CTA per email** — every added ask imposes choice overload, and the busy reader defaults to "none of the above."
5. **Keep it short: ~50–125 words, target 60–90, 3–5 sentences** — length signals "I'm about to sell you" and gets deferred (= never answered). Brevity reads as a peer note. *(Cross-source direction is solid; treat exact reply percentages as illustrative.)*
6. **Frame the cost of inaction, not the upside** — loss is felt ~2x as strongly as an equal gain (prospect theory), and B2B buyers are risk-minimizers. Convert "you'll gain X" → "you're losing X" — but only with a *real*, signal-derived loss, never an invented one.
7. **Sell to the person, not just the P&L** — buyers act on personal/career value (looking good to the board, de-risking a decision they own) more than on company ROI. Add one line aimed at *their* standing, mapped to their role. *(Google/CEB: personal value ~2x the impact, ~50% more likely to buy — supported.)*
8. **Match social proof to the recipient's exact segment, with a sharp number** — a peer who is demonstrably *like them* (same industry/size/role) is the strongest risk-reducer; "Northwind cut ramp 90→38 days" beats "trusted by 500+." Specificity > scale.
9. **Don't pitch in the first email** — leading with your product asks the reader to evaluate something they didn't request; lead with their problem and a question. *(Gong, 28M emails: pitching cuts reply up to 57% — supported.)*
10. **Follow up; never send a single-touch campaign** — ~42–55% of replies come from follow-ups, and the first follow-up alone adds ~50–66% more replies. Each step must carry a NEW angle, not "just bumping this." *(Cross-source supported.)*
11. **For voice, open with rapport + a stated reason, never "did I catch you at a bad time?"** — that opener pre-loads an easy exit (0.9% success). Giving a reason licenses the interruption. *(Gong, 90,380 calls — supported.)*
12. **Strip AI tells and obey structural deliverability limits** — em-dash overuse, "delve/leverage/robust," "I hope this finds you well," 2+ links, attachments, ALL-CAPS all read as bulk automation and erode the one thing cold email sells: that a human did the work.

## 2. Anatomy of a High-Performing Cold Email

Target: **60–90 words, 3–5 sentences, grade 5–8 reading level, one CTA, one link max, plain text.**

| Part | Job | DO | DON'T |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Subject** | Win the open without a sales tell | 1–4 words OR a short specific phrase, lowercase, tied to the trigger ("warehouse hiring", "your SDR ramp page"). Sentence case, no emoji. | ALL-CAPS, numbers, buzzwords, fake "Re:/Fwd:", first-name-only tokens, a full marketing sentence. *(Note: short-lowercase optimizes opens; Backlinko's reply data slightly favors descriptive — treat as a trade-off, default short.)* |
| **Opener (line 1)** | Prove "this is not a blast" in <20 words | ONE public, verifiable trigger: "Saw Northwind just opened a Denver office and posted two RevOps roles." | A greeting platitude, a compliment ("loved your post"), "I hope this finds you well," or your own company. |
| **Relevance (line 2)** | Connect the trigger to a plausible implication | "Usually means the CRM's about to get messy." One sentence. Optionally hedge ("not sure if this is on your radar yet") to lower defensiveness. | A research dump or a second stacked signal — past one point, extra detail reads as scraping. |
| **Value** | Quantified outcome for *their* segment, not a feature | "[Similar peer] cut [metric] from X to Y in Z." Sharp number, named/descriptor peer, optional one career-stake line. | Feature lists, superlatives, "we help companies X," round marketing numbers ("save thousands"). |
| **CTA** | One soft, frictionless ask | Interest/permission question, ≤15 words, second-to-last line: "Worth a 2-line reply?" / "Open to me sending the teardown?" | A meeting/calendar/15-min ask, a Calendly link, two asks stacked. |
| **P.S. (optional)** | Reinforce the trigger or proof | One line restating the strongest proof or trigger relevance. | A second, competing CTA (violates one-ask). |

**Signature:** name, role @ company, ONE LinkedIn link (legitimacy signal, ~+10% reply, correlational). No image blocks, no second link.

## 3. Worked Examples

### Example A — Trigger-based (stacked signal, low pain-awareness → PAS-lite)

> **Subject:** scaling the SDR team
>
> Jennifer — saw the Q4 note about doubling the sales team, and four new SDR roles went up last week. *[Reasoning: two signals pointing at ONE initiative — earnings language + hiring — woven into one line. Not a stack of unrelated facts.]*
>
> Usually when outbound scales that fast, account research becomes the bottleneck before the headcount pays off — new reps burn weeks learning prioritization while the best signal windows close. *[Reasoning: plausible-implication sentence = the cost-of-inaction loss frame, derived from the real signal, no product yet.]*
>
> When you scaled outbound at Segment, did research turn into the choke point? *[Reasoning: ONE genuine question, no pitch, references her own history — a micro-yes that costs nothing.]*
>
> — Marcus Reyes · RevOps @ Flowstack · linkedin.com/in/marcusreyes

*Why it works: 48 words, one initiative, loss-framed implication, soft interest CTA, zero feature mention.*

### Example B — Pain-based (segment fallback when no individual signal exists → matched social proof)

> **Subject:** five tools, one source of truth
>
> Hi Dana — most heads of RevOps at 200–300-person SaaS shops tell me the same thing: the data's in five tools and none of them agree. *[Reasoning: NO fresh event signal available, so this is honestly segment-level — persona pain + size tier, not faked 1:1. Expect ~5–9%, not ~18%.]*
>
> Northwind (also ~250-person SaaS) got theirs into one clean source and cut SDR ramp from 90 to 38 days. *[Reasoning: matched-peer proof on industry+size, sharp non-round number, not the biggest logo.]*
>
> Is that five-tool reality still the case on your side, or already wrangled? *[Reasoning: one binary-ish interest question, easy to answer in a sentence.]*
>
> — Marcus Reyes · RevOps @ Flowstack · linkedin.com/in/marcusreyes

*Why it works: 52 words, honest about its tier, matched proof, frictionless ask.*

### Example C — Cold-call opener (reuses the email's anchor signal)

> "Hey Marcus, how have you been? … I know I'm catching you out of the blue. The reason I'm calling — I saw you posted four SDR roles this week right after the Series B, and when teams scale on-call hiring that fast, account research usually turns into the bottleneck. Can I take 30 seconds to tell you why that made me pick up the phone, and you can tell me to get lost?"

*Reasoning: rapport opener ("How have you been?" — Gong's top performer, 10.01%) → stated reason ("the reason I'm calling," 2.1x lift) → the SAME trigger used in the email → a second-line permission micro-ask with a no-license ("tell me to get lost"). Never opens with "did I catch you at a bad time?"*

## 4. Personalization Rubric (signals ranked by response lift → sentence rule)

Use the **15-5-1 discipline**: roughly 15 signals surfaced → 5 qualify → 1 gets a full 1:1, 4 get company-level. Cap personalized-tier contacts at **1–2 per account** (Belkins: 1 contact 7.8% vs 10+ contacts 3.8% reply — supported). Stack at most **3 signals, and only if they point at the SAME initiative.**

| Rank | Signal | Why it converts | Sentence rule (encodable) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Leadership / job change** | New mandate, fresh budget, no incumbent loyalty | Reference the *mandate*, not congratulations: "New owners of a pipeline number usually get ~90 days before the board wants a trajectory — what's the first lever?" Fire within 24–48h. |
| 2 | **Funding round** | Urgency to spend on growth | Name recency + implied spend: "Saw the Series B last week — usually the quarter teams rebuild outbound." |
| 3 | **Hiring / job postings** | Reveals the active initiative and its pain | "The four SDR roles you posted point to scaling outbound — research usually becomes the choke point." |
| 4 | **Earnings / transformation language** | Public statement of priority | Quote the initiative: "Your Q4 note about doubling the team…" |
| 5 | **Product launch / expansion / new office** | Operational strain incoming | "Northwind just opened a Denver office — usually the CRM gets messy next." |
| 6 | **Tech-stack change** | Predictable downstream headache | Hedge it: "Not sure if this is on your radar, but you just rolled out Snowflake — the next headache is usually reverse-ETL into the CRM." |
| 7 | **Public LinkedIn post / conference talk / podcast** | Stated opinion you can join | Join the conversation, don't flatter: react to the *idea*, never "loved your post." |
| — | **Segment-only (role + size + industry)** | Fallback, label honestly | "Most heads of RevOps at 200–300-person SaaS…" — route here when no event exists; do NOT dress as 1:1. |

**Hard rules:** Whitelist PUBLIC, PROFESSIONAL sources only. **Blacklist** personal/family/location/lifestyle data, email-open/click behavior, geolocation, anything requiring login or scraping — it trips the surveillance/creepiness threshold and builds distrust. One professional relevance point per email; never more than two. Personal-life references = zero.

## 5. The CTA Menu (ranked by friction → when to use)

Gate every CTA on **lead temperature**. Escalate commitment only as the lead warms.

| Friction | CTA type | Example | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lowest | **Permission / one-word yes** | "Worth a look, or not a priority right now? Either answer helps." | Maximum reply volume; busy senior buyers; breakup touches |
| Low | **Interest question** | "Is keeping ramp under 60 days something your team's wrestling with this quarter?" | **Default cold first touch** |
| Low–mid | **Offer-based (named asset)** | "Want the 1-page teardown of how Brex killed 6 hrs/week of reconciliation? No call needed." | When you have a real, relevant asset + any hook. Best with personalization. *(30MPC: +28% vs baseline — supported; the "4x vs interest" claim is dropped.)* |
| Mid | **Soft conversation ask** | "Worth 15 minutes to compare notes?" | Touch 3+, or after light engagement |
| High | **Two-option anchored time** | "Tuesday 2pm or Thursday 10am ET — or send a time that's easier." Always include timezone + one calendar link. | **ONLY after a positive reply / active eval.** Never on first touch. *(Gong: specific-time wins at deal stage, 37% booking.)* |
| Recovery | **Breakup / file-close** | "Should I close your file? Reply 1 (worth a chat), 2 (not now), 3 (not a fit)." | Final touch after 3–5 unanswered |

**Rules:** exactly ONE ask per email; ≤15 words; second-to-last line; never an open-ended "let me know when you're free"; never a calendar link in a cold first email.

## 6. Anti-Pattern Checklist — NEVER do this

**Personalization & relevance**
- [ ] Treating `{{first_name}}`/`{{company}}` as personalization
- [ ] Wikipedia/flattery openers ("leading provider of…", "loved your post")
- [ ] Referencing private/personal-life or behavioral data ("saw you opened this Tuesday from Denver") — surveillance, not care
- [ ] 3+ personalization points or a research dump
- [ ] Stale signals referenced weeks late (buying window closed)
- [ ] Blasting 10+ contacts at one account with the "same" template
- [ ] Stacking *unrelated* signals (award + tech change + hire that don't connect)

**Structure & CTA**
- [ ] A meeting/calendar/15-min ask on first touch
- [ ] Multiple stacked CTAs
- [ ] Pitching a product/feature in the first email
- [ ] Over 125 words / single dense block / grade 14+ prose
- [ ] Gain-only framing with no cost-of-inaction
- [ ] Round "marketing" numbers in an analytical email ("save thousands," "300+ companies")
- [ ] Single-email campaigns; "just following up" filler follow-ups
- [ ] Fabricated urgency / invented competitors / "limited spots"

**Voice**
- [ ] Opening with "did I catch you at a bad time?" or a bare greeting
- [ ] One-sentence pitch blocks (under-pitching reads as low conviction; aim ~40–55s)
- [ ] Starting an objection rebuttal with "But…/Actually…" — agree first
- [ ] Complying with "send me an email" and hanging up
- [ ] 3+ voicemails (drops email reply below baseline); voicemails over ~20–30s

**AI-generated tells (final style pass — reject the draft if present)**
- [ ] "I hope this email finds you well / I wanted to reach out / I came across your profile"
- [ ] Words: delve, leverage (verb), robust, pivotal, seamless, elevate, unlock, synergy, tapestry, testament, game-changer
- [ ] More than 1 em-dash (prefer 0; split into short sentences)
- [ ] Rule-of-three parallel lists ("speed, efficiency, and innovation")
- [ ] "Not only… but also…"

**Deliverability (build-time validators)**
- [ ] 2+ links, any attachment, HTML/image template, ALL-CAPS, 2+ exclamation marks
- [ ] Spam-promise words: free, guaranteed, act now, limited time, urgent, risk-free, click here, buy now
- [ ] Broken/empty merge field or "Hi there," fallback

## 7. Encode This Into lysoreach

**Generation rules (the engine enforces automatically):**

1. **Tiering gate.** Require ≥1 event-anchored relevance line before labeling an email "personalized." No fresh signal → drop to segment template, label it `segment-level`, route to lighter tier, expect ~5–9% not ~18%.
2. **Single-signal opener, ≤20 words, public-source only.** Pull from the whitelist (funding, hire, job post, earnings, launch, public post). Blacklist personal/behavioral/scraped data at the source layer.
3. **Signal scorer + stacker.** Weight leadership/job-change > funding > hiring > earnings > launch > tech-stack > social post. Stack ≤3 only if they share one initiative. Attach a **freshness decay**: Tier-1 signals fire within 24–48h or downgrade; suppress signals past their window so copy never references stale events.
4. **Account cap.** ≤1–2 contacts/account on personalized tier; 10+ contacts → route to segment template + flag for human selection of the single best contact.
5. **Length + readability validators.** Reject/regenerate first-touch >125 words or >8 sentences; target 60–90 words, grade 5–8. Strip backstory/credentials first when cutting.
6. **One-CTA + temperature gate.** Count distinct asks; reject if >1. CTA type = f(lead_stage): cold → interest/permission; engaged → offer-based; in-eval → two-option time + one link. Never emit a calendar link cold.
7. **Loss-frame pass.** Generate a cost-of-inaction variant before a gain variant, using a quantified, signal-derived pain metric. Never fabricate the loss.
8. **Personal-value line.** Append one role-mapped standing line (quota for VP Sales, defensible numbers for CFO, fewer fires for Head of Ops).
9. **Matched-peer proof.** Pull the comparator from the lead's own segment (industry+size+role); prefer sharp non-round numbers; one proof point, never a list.
10. **AI-tell + structural validator (final gate).** Reject on denylisted phrases/words, >1 em-dash, rule-of-three, 2+ links, attachments, ALL-CAPS, 2+ "!", spam-promise words. Validate every merge token; on null → graceful drop or hold the email (never "Hi there,").
11. **Sequence builder.** Never single-touch. Default 3–5 follow-up steps (4–7 total touches, hard cap 7), each with a NEW angle. Space 3–4 days, widening gaps. Weekdays only, no weekend first sends, midweek follow-up push.
12. **Multichannel coherence.** Reuse the SAME anchor signal across email + voicemail + LinkedIn + call. Voice opener = rapport line + "the reason I'm calling" + same trigger + permission micro-ask. Voicemail ≤20–30s, benefit-led, "no need to call back — I'll email," cap 2. LinkedIn-connect-first to warm the email when available.

**Evaluation rules (A/B scoring):** Calibrate to real benchmarks — average reply **~3.4%**, good 5–10%, elite 10.7%+. Optimize to **reply / positive-reply, NOT open rate** (Apple MPP inflates opens). Require a meaningful lift over the ~3.4% baseline before promoting any template. Deliverability ceilings: spam complaints <0.3%, bounce <2%; auto-pause the batch if >10% of merge tags hit a fallback.

**A note on the numbers:** the engine should treat the *directions* here as load-bearing and the *magnitudes* skeptically. The fact-check flagged the headline "5.2x signal-personalization lift" as a cross-stitch of two studies (real lift ~2–2.5x), the autobound "25–40% reply" tiers as single-vendor marketing well above the Belkins ~7.8% real-world ceiling, and the "287% multichannel" and "44% time-ask penalty" figures as misattributed or untraceable. The robust, cross-source spine you can build on: **brevity, one trigger, soft CTA, follow up, match the peer, frame the loss, don't pitch first, and never sound like a bot.**
