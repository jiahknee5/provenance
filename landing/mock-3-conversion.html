// how it works

Understand the apt → build to fit → prove → optimize.

Four moves turn a click into a page that’s apt on both counts: perfectly suited to the visitor, and one they’re likely to convert on.

STEP01

Understand the apt

Resolve each visitor into a live segment from source, firmographics, intent signals, and your CRM. Apt models what this person is apt to want — and what they’re apt to ignore.

STEP02

Build the page to fit

From your claims library, Apt assembles the headline, proof points, offer, and CTA most fitted to that segment — a page written for one visitor, not stretched over all of them.

STEP03

Prove every claim

Before anything ships, the Provenance Gate decomposes each claim and demands a cited source. Unprovable claims are killed, not published — personalization that never risks a false promise.◆ cited or killed

STEP04

Optimize inside the truth

A truth-bounded optimizer runs variants and keeps whatever lifts CTA — but only among claims already cleared by the Gate. It learns the message each segment is apt to convert on, and re-verifies when the underlying facts change.
