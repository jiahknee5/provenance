# Apt — Landing Page Mocks &amp; Pitch Deck

Landing-page concept for **Provenance**, built around the two dictionary senses of **apt**:

> **apt** *(adjective)*
> 1. appropriate — **perfectly fitted** to the circumstances.
> 2. having a tendency — **inclined &amp; likely** to act.

**The thesis:** *Make a page **apt** for every visitor, and every visitor becomes **apt** to convert.*

- **Problem** — static landing pages speak to an "average visitor" who doesn't exist, so they reach no one and leak paid traffic.
- **Solution** — hyper-personalized landing pages that raise **CTA**, lower **CAC**, and lift **ROI** — with every claim cited or killed by the Provenance Gate.
- **How it works** — Understand the apt → Build to fit → Prove every claim → Optimize inside the truth.
