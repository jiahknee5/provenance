Glossary without the statistics lecture

PriorStarting belief before results arrive.
PosteriorUpdated belief after observed results.
Credible intervalRange of CTR values the model considers plausible.
P(Best)Chance an arm beats every active competitor in posterior draws.
EntropyHow undecided the ranking remains.
KL divergenceHow far belief moved from its starting point.
RegretSimulator-only expected reward lost while learning.
VarianceSpread of a belief, not quality of an arm.
TruthHidden simulator setting; unknown in production.

5

Ranges: heuristic, not law

Continue normally

List burn below 0.50%, approved claims, no data-quality warning.

Investigate

Wide intervals, high entropy after substantial traffic, isolated unsubscribe spike.

Act

Unapproved claim eligible, sustained list burn above threshold, broken event counts.

Demo heuristics are operational prompts, not universal statistical significance thresholds. Business risk and traffic volume still govern action.

Worked outcomes

Expand the event you need to debug or explain.

Meeting booked: why the curve moves right

A success increments alpha. The posterior mean usually rises, the arm may win more Monte Carlo comparisons, and future traffic may increase.

Ordinary miss: why the estimate moves left

A miss increments beta by one. The model becomes more skeptical without applying the stronger unsubscribe penalty.

Unsubscribe: why one event has extra weight

An unsubscribe increments beta by three. This is a business-utility penalty, equivalent to three negative pseudo-observations, not a standard Bernoulli update.

Archive or Gate mask: why rankings can change without an outcome

Removing an arm changes the comparison set. P(Best) and entropy can change immediately even though no new conversion evidence arrived.

Know what kind of value you are reading

ObservedEstimatedSimulator-only
ObservedSends, meetings, unsubscribes. Events that happened.
EstimatedCTR, intervals, P(Best), entropy, KL. Computed beliefs.
Simulator-onlyTruth and regret. Available because the demo knows hidden parameters.
Key ruleNever present an estimate as an observation.
Production ruleNever expect truth or true regret to be available.

10

Common mistakes

P(Best) is not CTRP(Best) compares arms; estimated CTR describes one arm's expected conversion rate.
Higher KL is not betterKL measures learning distance. The model may have learned that an arm is poor.
Lower variance is not betterIt means greater certainty, which can be certainty about weak performance.
Leader is not settledA current leader can still have a wide interval and limited evidence.
Aggregate CTR cannot rank armsAdaptive allocation and mixed segments make the global rate unsuitable for arm comparison.
