Why the gate is the moat — not the bandit, not de-anon

Every primitive is commoditized; the enforced combination is not

De-anon is not novel

Mutiny, Warmly, RB2B, Vector, Qualified all resolve visitors live. We add the tokenized-link → individual tier (Mutiny/most are company-level only).

Bandits are not novel

Optimizely (Contextual MABs) and Dynamic Yield ship them. Ours selects a personalization tier under a creepiness ceiling — which none surface.

Generation is not novel

Ploy, Mutiny, Optimizely Opal, VWO Copilot, HubSpot Breeze, 6sense all generate copy. None gate it against a verifiable source.

The enforced truth-gate IS novel

Across 16 companies the "trust" stories are statistical significance, brand-consistency, or "grounded + a human reviewer." None withholds an unprovable claim by construction. None shows a receipt.

Mutiny generates ROI calculators with no source trace. HubSpot Breeze ships generated landing pages. 6sense sends "grounded" emails a human skims.
We can't say anything we can't prove — and we show the receipt.
The white space, stress-tested

The market is racing toward the exact danger our gate removes

The "trust" language already in market is not claim provenance:

Optimizely / VWO: statistical significance — a variant won, not that a claim is true Mutiny: "grounded in brand" — sounds like us, not provable 6sense / Opal / Copilot: "grounded + human review" — a reviewer, not a gate Attio / Clay: advisory citations — descriptive, internal, never withholds UNCLAIMED → enforced provenance + visible receipts + creepiness ceiling, at render

HubSpot Breeze and 6sense are wiring LLMs into customer-facing generation with no verification layer. 11x already drew a public scandal — fabricated customers, a prospect congratulated on a 3-year-stale funding round — for exactly the unverified-claim failure our gate prevents. As generation scales, "verifiable claim" moves from nicety to procurement/legal requirement — acutely for our Helix B2B-healthcare tenant, where SOC2/HIPAA/MLR holds already gate claims. That's the tailwind; the gate is the moat.

So what

Six moves this read implies

  1. Fight in Ring 1, against Mutiny and Ploy; partner-frame Rings 2–3. Beat the entrenched #1 (Mutiny: logos, $72M, deep plumbing) on trust, not personalization breadth.
  2. Lead every demo with the receipt. Same personalized page — ours with a source-tagged verification receipt, theirs without. Sharpest against Mutiny's ROI calculators, Breeze's generated pages, 6sense's "grounded" emails.
  3. Use the foils. Warmly = aggressive de-anon, no creepiness ceiling (governance contrast). 11x = the live accuracy scandal. Both make the gate concrete.
  4. Don't claim the bandit or de-anon as the moat. They exist elsewhere. The moat is the enforced truth-gate + receipts + tiered creepiness ceiling — and the combination.
  5. Integrate the data layer. Attio (CRM lookup + receipt write-back), RB2B/Vector (identity inputs), Clay (enrichment), Haus/Measured (downstream lift proof) are partnerships, not fights.
  6. Move now. Ploy raised $27M in months; Qualified→Salesforce; Mutiny pivoting; HubSpot/6sense shipping generation. The truth-gate is durable because it's the hard part everyone is skipping.
