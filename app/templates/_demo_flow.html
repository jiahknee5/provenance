How the demo works

Every visitor sees a page personalized to who they are — and the page only says what we can prove, each line carrying a receipt. Follow the flow:

Visitor tour What a prospect experiences
1StartThe demo sitemap — /apt/demo
→

↓ On the live page, click the receipt chip to go behind the scenes and see why every choice was made
Behind the scenes What you show to explain it

→
7Marketer consoleThe business view: copy decisions, delivery, and attribution

One rule of thumb: channels differ by how much we know the moment a visitor arrives.

Search+ search intent from the referrer
Ads+ the full campaign — UTMs & ad variant
